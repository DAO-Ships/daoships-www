# Set Up Your Wallet

Before you can launch a ship or vote on a proposal, you need a wallet that speaks Quai Network. This guide gets you from zero to connected in a few minutes. By the end, you'll have a funded wallet on the Cyprus-1 testnet and the [DAO Ships app](https://app.daoships.xyz) recognizing you.

<Callout type="info" title="What you'll need">
A desktop browser (Chrome, Brave, or Firefox), about five minutes, and nothing else. Testnet QUAI is free.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Install Pelagus

Pelagus is the recommended wallet for Quai Network — think of it as the Quai-native equivalent of a browser wallet extension.

1. Visit [pelaguswallet.io](https://pelaguswallet.io) and add the extension to your browser.
2. Pin it to your toolbar so it's easy to find.
3. Open the extension to start setup.

<Callout type="tip" title="Already on a different wallet?">
DAO Ships also supports WalletConnect, so you can connect a mobile or hardware wallet that supports Quai. Pelagus is the smoothest path, and the rest of this guide assumes it.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Create or import a wallet

When you first open Pelagus, you'll choose between creating a new wallet or importing an existing one.

1. To **create** a wallet, follow the prompts and write down your recovery phrase on paper.
2. To **import**, paste your existing recovery phrase.
3. Set a strong password to unlock the extension on this device.

<Callout type="danger" title="Guard your recovery phrase">
Your recovery phrase is the only way to restore your wallet. Never type it into a website, never share it, and never store it in plain text online. Anyone who has it controls your funds.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Switch to Cyprus-1 testnet

Quai is a sharded network, and DAO Ships currently runs on the **Cyprus-1** zone of the testnet.

1. Open Pelagus and find the network selector.
2. Choose the testnet, then select the **Cyprus-1** zone (chain id `15000`).
3. Confirm your active address starts with `0x00` — that prefix marks a valid Cyprus-1 address.

If you don't see the testnet, check Pelagus settings to enable test networks.

## Step 4: Get testnet QUAI

You need a little QUAI to pay gas for transactions like launching a ship or casting a vote. Testnet QUAI has no real-world value and is free from a faucet.

1. Copy your Cyprus-1 address from Pelagus.
2. Visit the [Quai faucet](https://faucet.quai.network) and paste your address.
3. Request testnet QUAI and wait a moment for it to arrive.
4. Refresh Pelagus to confirm a balance appears.

<Callout type="tip" title="How much is enough?">
A small balance covers many transactions. Launching a ship costs a bit more gas than a vote, so grab a comfortable cushion while you're at the faucet.
</Callout>

## Step 5: Connect to the app

Now bring it all together.

1. Go to [app.daoships.xyz](https://app.daoships.xyz).
2. Click **Connect Wallet** in the header.
3. Choose **Pelagus** (or **WalletConnect** if you're using that path).
4. Approve the connection request in your wallet.

Once connected, the app shows your address and balance. You're ready to set sail.
